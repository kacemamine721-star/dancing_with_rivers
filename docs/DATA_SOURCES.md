Raw downloads are cached under `cache/context/<source>/`; analysis copies are in `work/` and `work/context/*.gpkg` (EPSG:32632); web copies are in `apps/web/public/data/2026-11-15/` and registered in `manifest.json`. Run results, counts and checks: `work/context/report.md`.

## Notes

- **Not legal boundaries.** geoBoundaries and the derived Tunisia–Algeria line are indicative; the two national datasets are not edge-matched.
- **Badges.** OSM and geoBoundaries are mapped/reported information (REPORTED). HydroSHEDS, JRC GSW and WorldCover are satellite/DEM-derived products (MEASURED, derived). WorldPop is a model (MODELLED).
- **Planetary Computer** (https://planetarycomputer.microsoft.com/) hosts JRC GSW and WorldCover; its terms of use apply to access, the dataset licences above apply to the data.
- **References.** Lehner, B., Grill, G. (2013) Hydrological Processes 27(15) doi:10.1002/hyp.9740 · Pekel, J.-F. et al. (2016) Nature 540, 418–422 · Zanaga, D. et al. (2022) ESA WorldCover 10 m 2021 v200, doi:10.5281/zenodo.7254221 · Bondarenko, M. et al. (2020) WorldPop constrained estimates, doi:10.5258/SOTON/WP00684 · Runfola, D. et al. (2020) PLoS ONE 15(4) e0231866.
